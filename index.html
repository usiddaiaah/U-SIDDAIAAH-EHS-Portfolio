Core Industry Expertise

1. Cement Manufacturing Safety

Integrated cement plants, grinding units, kiln/preheater maintenance, and silo safety.

  • Extensive EHS experience in integrated cement plants, grinding units, and manufacturing operations, covering production, maintenance, electrical, mechanical, and utility areas.
  • Safety management for high-risk activities including kiln and preheater maintenance, raw mill operations, clinker handling, conveyor systems, crusher areas, packing plants, and material handling systems.
  • Strong expertise in confined space and silo entry, including permit-to-work, gas testing, continuous monitoring, ventilation, standby arrangements, rescue planning, and emergency preparedness.
  • Effective implementation of LOTO/LOTOTO, PTW, HIRA, JSA, Dynamic Risk Assessment (DRA), and Critical Risk Management for routine and non-routine activities.
  • Robust control of work at height, hot work, lifting operations, machine guarding, electrical safety, fire prevention, and contractor safety.
  • Development and implementation of initiatives such as Safety Zone Owners, Safety Conversations, Cardinal Rules, Consequence Management, and “No Guard – No Start” to strengthen safety culture.
  • Experience in safety audits, statutory compliance, incident investigation, corrective/preventive actions, emergency response, and continuous improvement.

2. Alternative Fuels & Raw Materials (AFR)

Hazardous waste co-processing, combustible storage, chemical exposure, and spill response.

  • EHS management for AFR receiving, unloading, storage, transportation, feeding, handling, and co-processing operations within cement manufacturing environments.
  • Risk assessment and control of hazards associated with hazardous waste, combustible materials, chemical exposure, dust, toxic gases/vapours, fire and explosion risks.
  • Implementation of HIRA/JSA, PTW, SDS-based chemical controls, PPE requirements, spill response, fire prevention, and emergency preparedness.
  • Safety controls for material handling systems, feeding systems, storage areas, conveyors, pumps, and associated mechanical/electrical equipment.
  • Development of spill containment, fire-risk mitigation, toxic-gas monitoring, housekeeping, and waste-handling procedures.
  • Contractor and operator competency development through induction, toolbox talks, safety training, mock drills, and competency assessments.

3. Waste Heat Recovery Systems (WHRS)

WHR power generation, high-pressure boilers, turbines, ducting, and commissioning safety.

  • Specialized EHS experience in WHR power plant projects and operations, including boiler, turbine, generator, AQC/PH systems, hot-gas ducting, steam systems, and associated utilities.
  • Safety management for high-pressure boilers, steam lines, turbine-generator areas, pressure systems, hot surfaces, thermal exposure, rotating equipment, and high-temperature gases.
  • Expertise in safety during WHR project erection, commissioning, synchronization, turbine rolling, boiler activities, hydro testing, inspections, and start-up operations.
  • Risk management for hot-gas ducting, structural erection, heavy lifting, work at height, confined spaces, welding/cutting, electrical work, and commissioning activities.
  • Implementation of PSSR, HIRA, JSA, PTW, LOTOTO, emergency shutdown preparedness, fire protection, rescue planning, and commissioning safety protocols.
  • Experience coordinating with project, O&M, engineering, contractors, OEMs, statutory authorities, and senior management to ensure safe project execution.
  • Proven WHR safety performance, including achievement of millions of safe man-hours without LTI and recognition for safety performance.

4. Civil, Structural & Heavy Construction Safety

Greenfield & brownfield construction, scaffolding, heavy lifting, and excavation safety.

  • Comprehensive EHS management for greenfield and brownfield industrial projects, including civil construction, structural erection, mechanical installation, electrical works, commissioning, and project handover.
  • Strong expertise in scaffolding safety, work at height, fall protection, lifelines, platform safety, edge protection, access/egress, and structural integrity.
  • Safety management of excavation, deep excavation, shoring, formwork, rebar activities, concreting, structural fabrication and erection.
  • Competent control of crane and lifting operations, including lifting plans, crane inspection, rigging arrangements, rigger competency, lifting accessories, exclusion zones, and critical lifts.
  • Heavy equipment and vehicle safety through traffic management plans, designated routes, reversing controls, banksman/spotter systems, speed management, and equipment inspections.
  • Experience in structural and mechanical erection involving boilers, ducts, platforms, conveyors, equipment installation, heavy components, and industrial structures.
  • Strong focus on contractor safety management, including prequalification, induction, competency verification, supervision, performance monitoring, audits, safety scoring, and consequence management.

ISO-aligned EHS frameworks, HIRA/JSA, LOTO, PSSR, and Fatal Risk controls.

  • Experienced in developing SOPs, SWPs, risk registers, emergency response plans, inspection checklists, safety campaigns, training modules, and corrective-action tracking systems.
